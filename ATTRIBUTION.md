# BlockFactor: Open Data Attribution & Third-Party Notices

BlockFactor is built with open data and public APIs provided by municipal, transit, and open mapping organizations. We gratefully acknowledge the following data sources:

---

## 1. OpenStreetMap (OSM)
* **Data Provider:** [OpenStreetMap Contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/)
* **License:** [Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/)
* **Attribution Notice:** Contains information from OpenStreetMap, which is made available here under the Open Database License (ODbL).
* **Cartography & Map Tiles:** Vector tiles provided via CartoCDN / OpenFreeMap styles.

---

## 2. City of Chicago Data Portal
* **Data Provider:** City of Chicago ([data.cityofchicago.org](https://data.cityofchicago.org/))
* **Datasets Used:**
  * CTA 'L' (Rail) Stations & Lines (Shapefiles & Points)
  * Chicago Bike Routes & Protected Lanes
  * Chicago Parks & Recreation Facilities
  * Chicago Police Department Reported Crime Incidents
  * Chicago 311 Service Requests (Water & Sewer Complaints)
* **Terms of Use:** Data is provided for public use under the City of Chicago Open Data Terms of Use.

---

## 3. Chicago Transit Authority (CTA)
* **Data Provider:** Chicago Transit Authority ([transitchicago.com](https://www.transitchicago.com/developers/))
* **Datasets Used:** General Transit Feed Specification (GTFS) Static Schedules.
* **Developer License:** Used in compliance with the CTA Transit Data Terms of Use.

---

## 4. Divvy / Lyft Micro-Mobility
* **Data Provider:** Divvy / Lyft Urban Mobility ([divvybikes.com](https://divvybikes.com/system-data))
* **Datasets Used:** General Bikeshare Feed Specification (GBFS) v2.3 feeds for real-time station capacity and locations.
* **License:** Provided under the GBFS Open Data license agreement.

---

## 5. Federal Communications Commission (FCC)
* **Data Provider:** FCC National Broadband Map ([broadbandmap.fcc.gov](https://broadbandmap.fcc.gov/))
* **Datasets Used:** Broadband serviceable location fabric and provider availability data.

---

## 6. Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA)
* **Data Provider:** FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer (NFHL) ([msc.fema.gov](https://msc.fema.gov/))
* **Datasets Used:** Flood Insurance Rate Maps (FIRM) flood hazard zones.
